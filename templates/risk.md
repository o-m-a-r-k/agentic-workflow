Judge how much harm this change could do if wrong, and what would make you sure it is not: what it does, what depends on it, how far a mistake spreads and how easily it is undone, test coverage, the project's invariants, your confidence. Money, irreversible external writes, lost data and access are examples, not a list.

Give `risk`: `level` low or high, `why`, and `needs` in proportion: `second-review`, `failing-test`, the person's `diff-read`, or a scenario to exercise.
