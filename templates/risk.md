Judge the harm this change could do if wrong, and what would make you sure it is not: what it does, what depends on it, how far a mistake spreads, how easily it is undone, test coverage, the project's invariants, your confidence. Money, irreversible external writes, retries, partial failures, lost data, access: examples, not a list.

Give `risk`: `level` low or high, `why`, and `needs` in proportion, spelled exactly: `second-review`; `failing-test`, if one can fail before the change; `diff-read`, if worth the person's time; or a check a reviewer can make by reading.
