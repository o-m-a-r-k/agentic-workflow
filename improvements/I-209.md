# I-209: Finding history (cause, not place) never reaches attempts admitted before 0.7.27

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.28
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-0728b.test.mjs`

## Failure

D7 kept 0.7.27's findingHistory out of a batch fix round handed under 0.7.27 because its members were admitted under 0.7.17/0.7.18; the same cause was found one place per round (3-4 rounds each for four causes). Information that only helps the implementer should reach live attempts (a relaxation, not a new obligation).

## Fix

Finding history goes to every implementer handed after a recorded round, in every attempt in flight, not only when the attempt's own finding blocks.
