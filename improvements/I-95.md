# I-95: Cross-repository lesson IDs can silently hide distinct lessons

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/lesson-identity.test.mjs` (two different lessons of one id reach the roles)

## Failure

In <ticket>, integrating two repositories' bases exposed lesson IDs allocated independently across repositories. loadLessons keys only by lower-case ID and silently retains the first repository's lesson. The incoming published targets of both repositories already both contain distinct L-1 lessons; a temporary L-4 repair lesson in one also hid the incoming L-4 lesson of the other. This attempt will namespace its own two repair lessons, preserving published lessons. The engine should detect distinct cross-repository collisions and use a stable repository-aware or globally unique allocation identity rather than silently omitting a lesson.

## Fix

Two repositories holding different lessons under one id keep both, as id@repository, with references that stay valid and nothing dropped silently; wf lesson add counts the lesson files of every attempt worktree when it allocates an id, and a lesson move never overwrites a different lesson of the same id.
