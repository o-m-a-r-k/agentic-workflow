# I-116: An attempt whose change shipped outside the workflow cannot be closed

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.5
- **Test:** `scenarios/close-shipped.test.mjs` (is closed as shipped under the person)

## Failure

An attempt's change reached the target branches outside wf (one repo recorded delivered, the others merged by hand), before its final evidence review; its worktrees and branches were then removed. wf abandon refuses an attempt with a delivered repo and wf deliver needs an acceptance, so the attempt stays ORPHANED in every status listing even after the person decided to close it as shipped without that review. A person-confirmed close as shipped (unverified), recorded with the decision and the open findings, is missing.

## Fix

wf close --shipped closes an attempt whose every recorded change git shows on its remote target branch, under the person's override-gate decision, with the commits and what was still open recorded; wf abandon of an orphan now refuses when its change landed.
