# I-208: Implementers 'fix the class' with redesigns that introduce new defects

- **Class:** template
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.28
- **Test:** `scenarios/course-signals.test.mjs`

## Failure

An implementer turned a finding whose reviewer-stated fix was 'compute the fingerprint before the provider call' into a 240-minute reservation/reconciliation redesign; an earlier one added a migration for 'one fingerprint over the whole command'. They produced the batch's only HIGH finding, four new findings and a re-plan adding 8 criteria; 9 of the ticket's 30 findings were regressions from the previous fix round. Goal: 'no review finds it again anywhere' is paired with 'the smallest change that removes the assumption'; a fix that needs a migration, a new state or a new endpoint is a re-plan request; an implementer's expect far above the reviewer's stated fix shows as a divergence at the round boundary.

## Fix

Reviewers state the fix and its minutes; implementers make the smallest change that removes the assumption and send a migration, new state or new endpoint as a re-plan request; an estimate far above the stated fix is a course signal.
