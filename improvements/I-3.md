# I-3: Delivered screenshots are visible in the ticket, not only attached

- **Class:** tracker
- **Fixed in:** 0.1.16
- **Test:** `scenarios/handoff-quality.test.mjs` (delivered handoff: summary required, screenshots embedded inline, raw readback only, anomalies recorded, screenshots viewable after close)

## Failure

Screenshots uploaded as attachments showed in the tracker only as "added N links"; nobody could see them on the ticket, and the delivered comment was a verbatim dump of every criterion.

## Fix

The engine renders the delivered comment (owner summary, user-visible UAT scope, each screenshot inline under its caption, known limits); the readback refuses a comment that does not embed every screenshot by its asset.
