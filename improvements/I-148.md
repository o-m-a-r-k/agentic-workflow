# I-148: Launch failures are unrecorded, a passing error is latched, and machine sleep reads as a stall

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.11
- **Test:** `scenarios/codex-run.test.mjs`

## Failure

Audit of 30 failed launches: none recorded why it ended; four that finished cleanly were recorded failed after a passing error event; a reviewer 94 minutes into its work was stopped as stalled 11 seconds after the machine woke from a 68-minute sleep.

## Fix

An error event fails a launch only when no turn completes after it; every failed launch records its kind, detail and signal; a role's quiet spell says how much of it the machine slept.
