# I-18: Every issue found during a ticket is fixed in that ticket; deferral needs the owner's recorded decision

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.4.4
- **Test:** `scenarios/discovered.test.mjs` (I-18: discovered issues are recorded)

## Failure

Implementers, reviewers and the owner agent repeatedly noted real defects found during a ticket (a pager sums only the current page, tables with no phone card view, an implicit page-size default, a failed Next that strands the user) and parked them as follow-ups or 'harmless today', without the owner deciding. Engine and roles should enforce: a discovered-issue ledger (wf discovered add/list/close) that any role can append to; each entry must end fixed (with a commit) or deferred with the owner's own words recorded; wf deliver refuses open entries; the reviewer bundle lists every entry and the reviewer gives each a verdict; implementer and reviewer role text says to report every issue found and never defer on their own; a scope or criterion that blocks the fix is amended by the owner, not used as the reason to defer.

## Fix

A discovered-issue ledger per attempt: wf discovered add, list and close. An entry closes only fixed by a commit of this ticket or deferred with the owner's own words (no words, or a role agent named, is refused). wf deliver refuses an open entry; the implementer and reviewer bundles list every entry and the closure needs a verdict per entry (fixed, deferred or open); acceptance refuses an open verdict and an entry recorded after the round was handed. Deferrals reach the delivered comment's known limits. Implementer, reviewer, work and quick-fix texts say to record every issue, never defer it on their own, and amend the criteria when they block the fix.

## Extension (same release)

Failure: an implementer reported "Not fixed, outside the brief: the status field shows the raw enum value" and handed the issue back to the owner agent to relay. Fix: the implementer, reviewer, work and quick-fix texts say the role that found an issue fixes it, in the same attempt, in whatever file it lives in, and the brief is never a reason to leave it; the only exception, a file another work item is editing at that moment, is recorded with `wf discovered add --blocked-by <work item>` and routed by the owner to that implementer; `wf handoff reviewer` refuses while an implementer's final report (from its Claude Code transcript) leaves an issue unfixed without naming its discovered entry. Test: `scenarios/discovered.test.mjs` (an implementer report that leaves an issue unfixed without a ledger entry is refused).

## Security fix (0.4.5)

Failure, found in review of 0.4.4: a deferral was refused only when the caller-typed `--by` named a role agent, so an agent that left `--by` out deferred with invented words. Fix: two owner channels. `wf discovered close --deferred` takes the decision from exactly one owner turn in the owner session's transcript that starts with the fixed phrase `defer <attempt>:<id>`, after an anchor (size and prefix sha256) recorded when the entry was added; the whole turn is recorded with its file, line, offset and anchor; no natural-language intent is judged. `wf discovered defer <id> --reason` runs only at an interactive terminal and needs the id typed (recorded unverified). Free text is refused; tool results, notifications, scheduled tasks, injected lines and subagent transcripts never count; `wf deliver` lists every deferral and refuses until the owner acknowledges each. Test: `scenarios/discovered.test.mjs` (I-18: discovered issues are recorded ...; 0.4.5: owner turns from a Codex rollout ...).
