# I-158: Provide a recorded owner decision for explicitly skipping a full gate

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.30
- **Test:** `scenarios/gate-light-only.test.mjs`

## Failure

Owner requested skipping full gate while retaining independent review. Engine supports gate-order and admission-definition overrides but no supported whole-gate waiver; owner cannot express this delivery decision without bypassing enforced acceptance. Provide a clear refusal and supported reserved decision route, or make the inability explicit in skill guidance.

## Fix

wf gate light-only --reason asks the person one reserved question naming what would not run and what passed; heavy steps are then skipped, the evidence reviewer gets them in notRun, and the delivered comment names them.
