# I-32: Verifier failures must retain assertion diagnostics

- **Class:** other
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.1
- **Test:** `scenarios/verify-output.test.mjs`

## Failure

The verifier reduces failed test output to test names and counters, discarding the assertion and stack trace. An intermittent failing tracker scenario therefore left no diagnostic evidence after its container exited.

## Fix

Print the complete output of failing verifier legs, retaining the assertion and stack trace. Passing legs remain summarized and the exit status still fails.
