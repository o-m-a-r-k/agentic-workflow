# I-169: Reconcile a defect recorded after its same-handoff repair with independent proof

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.19
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-0719.test.mjs`

## Failure

An implementer recorded a real defect after committing its repair, then retained failing-before and passing-after native execution. Close refuses the actual repair as already present on the recorded tree. Current reconciliation only supports low bookkeeping; a defect cannot be reconciled without a later artificial commit, misclassification, or separate workflow intervention. Provide an audited route requiring the original handoff range, repaired file, before/after proof and fresh independent reviewer confirmation; retain safeguards against stale fix claims. No bypass was performed.

## Fix

D27: a defect fixed before it was recorded closes on a reviewer's verification of that commit.
