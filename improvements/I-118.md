# I-118: A repair after a failed gate reran whole e2e suites to prove each fix

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.6
- **Test:** `scenarios/focused-repair.test.mjs` (given its facts, not a method)

## Failure

A gate failed in its two end-to-end steps (5 of 9,766 service tests; 612 of 1,197 browser tests), every failure reproducing on the base branch. 603 of the 612 were one cascade: a single spec's token-refresh storm revoked the shared test session, so later tests failed with 401. The owner briefed the repair with a method: to prove it with whole runs of both end-to-end steps "not only targeted specs", and nothing in the workflow said the gate after the fix is the proof. The implementer spent 130 of its 156 minutes inside 18 blocking waits: three whole browser-suite runs (31 and 22 minutes, a third stopped by the person), a whole service-suite run (16 minutes), two `wf check` runs, and a 13-minute single-spec run through the clean-stack command. The engine handed it none of the failed gate's facts (its bundle's gate field was empty), counted the browser failures as 448 (a test failing in several projects was counted once), and could not have rerun only the failed suites (the suite list came from JUnit only). The 150-minute estimate matched the imposed method and was not questioned.

## Fix

The gate after a repair is its proof: the implementer and owner texts say so, and the owner's brief carries what failed and the goal, not the runs. The repair implementer's bundle carries `failedGate` (the gate's tree and whether it is current, the whole failure count, the failed tests and suites, how long each failing step last ran, whether a step reruns only failed suites, failing files the change did not touch). Playwright JSON reports are suite lists, so `select` reruns only the failed spec files (a spec that failed to load counts as failed); a failing test is counted once per project; doctor names heavy steps that rerun whole and what they lack. `wf resume <item>` shows that item's attempt and `wf report --attempt` reports only that attempt (both ignored the name, and showed or listed other attempts as if they were the one asked for).
