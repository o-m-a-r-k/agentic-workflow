# I-118: A repair after a failed gate reran whole e2e suites to prove each fix

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.6
- **Test:** `scenarios/focused-repair.test.mjs` (given its facts, not a method)

## Failure

After a gate failed with 5 API and 50 portal e2e failures, the implementer handed the repair proved every fix by rerunning the whole suites (10 to 22 minutes each, eight times; 68 of its 113 minutes were waits). The steps could not rerun only failed suites: the engine builds a step's suite list from JUnit only, the Playwright step writes a JSON report the engine read only for failure names, and the Jest e2e config had no JUnit reporter. Nothing told the implementer which suites failed or the focused rerun route, nothing suggested a fresh environment when failures spread across areas the change never touched (50 of them vanished after a stack reset), and its 150-minute estimate for small fixes was not questioned.

## Fix

The repair implementer's bundle carries failedGate (what failed, step durations, whether a step reruns only failed suites, failing files the change did not touch); Playwright JSON reports are suite lists so select reruns only failed specs; doctor names heavy steps that rerun whole; the role texts state the goal, not a method.
