# I-118: A repair after a failed gate reran whole e2e suites to prove each fix

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.6
- **Test:** `scenarios/focused-repair.test.mjs` (given its facts, not a method)

## Failure

After a gate failed with 55 end-to-end failures across two suites, the implementer handed the repair proved every fix by rerunning the whole suites (10 to 22 minutes each, eight times; 68 of its 113 minutes were waits). The steps could not rerun only failed suites: the engine builds a step's suite list from JUnit only, the Playwright step writes a JSON report the engine read only for failure names, and the Jest e2e config had no JUnit reporter. Nothing gave the implementer the facts of the failed gate (which suites failed, how long each step ran, which failing files the change never touched: 50 of the failures vanished after the environment was reset), and its 150-minute estimate for small fixes was not questioned.

## Fix

The repair implementer's bundle carries failedGate (the gate's tree and whether it is current, what failed with whole counts, how long each failing step last ran, whether a step reruns only failed suites, failing files the change did not touch); Playwright JSON reports are suite lists so select reruns only failed specs; doctor names heavy steps that rerun whole; the role texts make the gate after the fix the repair's proof and state the implementer's goal (every failure fixed at its cause), not a method.
