# I-144: A launched reviewer's written closure is lost, and a briefly dirty tree ends a round

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.11
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-074-lifecycle.test.mjs`

## Failure

Audit of 51 attempts: three launched reviewers ended with their closure written and not recorded, and fresh reviewers judged the same tree; a closure refused while the worktree was briefly dirty on the same commits was redone by a fresh reviewer.

## Fix

When a launched reviewer ends without recording, the engine records the closure file it wrote, with every check; uncommitted changes on the reviewed commits refuse the closure without ending the round.
