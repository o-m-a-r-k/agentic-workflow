# I-33: Adoption rejects combined human requests

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.2
- **Test:** `scenarios/adoption.test.mjs` (adoption accepts an explicit command after reading the handover in the same human message)
- **Regression of:** I-31 (fixed in 0.5.1)

## Failure

An explicit request to read a handover and then adopt its ticket is refused because adoption must begin the message.

## Fix

Accept affirmative adoption command clauses after reading a handover, refuse quoted, reported, negated and conditional requests, and correct refusal diagnostics.
