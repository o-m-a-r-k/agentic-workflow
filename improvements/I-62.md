# I-62: Reviewer treats an indexed design reference as a required full-output receipt and stops with incomplete verdicts

- **Class:** template
- **Retired in:** 0.7.0
- **Refusals recorded:** none named

## Failure

A reviewer's bundle named a large indexed design-system reference (about 150 KB) among the documents it had to read. Two whole-file reads were truncated by the host's output limit; the reviewer stopped with its findings saved but its criterion, rule, lesson and impact verdict lists empty, and wf refused the closure for missing lesson verdicts. An earlier accepted round had read only the relevant indexed sections of the same reference.

## Why it was retired

Obsolete: 0.7.0 removed the reviewer read receipts and the rule, lesson and design verdict forms for every attempt (D6, a relaxation under D7), so no document is a required full-output read and an indexed design reference can no longer stop a review.
