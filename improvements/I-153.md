# I-153: A model at capacity fails a launch that could resume

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.30
- **Test:** `scenarios/codex-run.test.mjs`

## Failure

Audit of role launches: seven launches failed with server_overloaded (the model at capacity) and were replaced by fresh handoffs; four had made no edit.

## Fix

Each harness recognises its own capacity signal; the launcher resumes the same session with backoff, recorded as agent.launch.resumed; past the owner's return point the launch fails as capacity and wf handoff run resumes it.
