# I-1: Delivered screenshots are scoped to their own ticket

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.1.9, 0.1.10
- **Test:** `scenarios/screenshots.test.mjs` (batch: each member delivers only its own screenshots from the shared gate)

## Failure

A gate collected every capture its artifact globs matched, including other tickets' captures from earlier runs, and all of them were owed to the ticket.

## Fix

Artifact globs expand per ticket ({item}, {itemLower}); only files this run produced are collected; a batch member delivers only its own.
