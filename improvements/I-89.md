# I-89: Stopped unlaunched repair handoff consumes owed pattern sweeps

- **Class:** guard
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.4
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-074-lifecycle.test.mjs` (a repair handoff stopped before it ran does not spend its sweeps)

## Failure

A repair handoff with incomplete bare-directory sweep paths was closed stopped before native launch. The next handoff considered its findings already swept, although no implementation ran, so corrected sweeps could not be handed normally. Sweep obligation should not be spent by an unlaunched stopped handoff.

## Fix

a handoff closed stopped or failed spends no sweep (same fix as I-79)
