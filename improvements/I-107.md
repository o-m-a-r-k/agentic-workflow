# I-107: A criterion added after acceptance is delivered without evidence

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.4
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-074-lifecycle.test.mjs` (an amendment that adds a criterion withdraws the acceptance)

## Failure

wf criteria amend on an accepted attempt adds or changes frozen criteria but leaves the acceptance standing, so wf deliver delivers the attempt without the new criterion mapped to any evidence; wf accept would have refused it.

## Fix

from 0.7.4 an amendment that adds or re-words a criterion, or adds a repo, withdraws the acceptance on every attempt (D13; the event records it, so an amendment an earlier engine recorded folds unchanged and wf resume warns for it); wf accept refuses a criterion re-worded, or dropped and added again, after the round that mapped it; a batch is not delivered while a member's acceptance is withdrawn; the next step names the evidence review
