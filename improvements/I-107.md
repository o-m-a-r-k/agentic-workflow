# I-107: A criterion added after acceptance is delivered without evidence

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.4
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-074-lifecycle.test.mjs` (an amendment that adds a criterion withdraws the acceptance)

## Failure

wf criteria amend on an accepted attempt adds or changes frozen criteria but leaves the acceptance standing, so wf deliver delivers the attempt without the new criterion mapped to any evidence; wf accept would have refused it.

## Fix

wf criteria amend on an accepted attempt admitted at 0.7.4 or later withdraws the acceptance (the event records it; older ledgers fold unchanged); wf accept refuses a criterion re-worded after the round that mapped it; the next step names the evidence review; an earlier attempt keeps its acceptance and is warned what delivery would ship
