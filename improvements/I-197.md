# I-197: Reject malformed screenshot references when recording review and retain acceptance refusals

- **Class:** guard
- **Retired in:** 0.7.29
- **Refusals recorded:** none named

## Failure

Two independent evidence reviews copied combined screenshot paths/globs into a single criterion evidence field. wf review accepted the closures, but wf accept rejected C1/C4 later. A fresh handoff contained no acceptance-refusal context and repeated the same error. Validate exact artifact references at wf review and carry refusal details into the next review bundle to avoid repeated audits for a formatting defect.
