# I-7: Evidence is recorded, protected and verified at every use

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.1.20, 0.1.21
- **Test:** `scenarios/integrity.test.mjs` (attacks after the gate are refused at the next use: changed log, replaced screenshot, stray file, symlink, mode, truncated ledger, edited manifest, missing anchor)

## Failure

Writes through tools the guard cannot see (other connectors, other runtimes, gate steps, people) changed evidence unnoticed.

## Fix

Every evidence file in the hash-chained manifest, read-only and immutable where the platform allows, verified at every use, anchored outside the evidence.
