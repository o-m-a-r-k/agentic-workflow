# I-210: The round boundary never changes course: no strategist in 47 rounds

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.28
- **Test:** `scenarios/course-signals.test.mjs`

## Failure

The owner judged 'converging, not calling the strategist' six times while files ran 2-4x the plan (119 vs 62, 206 vs 48) and criteria were amended upward four times; no strategist was ever handed and the three round-10 escalations were answered 'continue'. A round-10 escalation whose answer was a product question ('what is the industry best practice here?') had no option for it and stayed open 390 minutes overnight. Goal: wf resume leads with handing the strategist when files exceed twice the plan and criteria were amended; the escalation offers 'this needs a product or legal answer: hold' so such a question has a route.

## Fix

The engine names course signals (files past twice the expectation, rounds past it, criteria added during implementation, a recurring cause, findings on lines the last fix wrote, an estimate far above the stated fix or a re-plan request) and makes the strategist the next step until a decision answers them; the round escalation offers hold for a product or legal answer.
