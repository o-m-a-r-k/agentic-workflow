# I-156: Investigate starvation of long-waiting runtime lease requests

- **Class:** engine
- **Retired in:** 0.7.29
- **Refusals recorded:** none named

## Failure

During <ticket> repair validation, a managed Docker request waited over 25 minutes while all three lease holders were alive and holder identities changed across checks. Investigate queue fairness and long-lived lease ownership; do not increase concurrency or stop foreign resources as a workaround. The delay kept an otherwise stable repair uncommitted pending required tests.

## Why it was retired

Duplicate of I-157 (fair queuing for lease waits), which stays open.
