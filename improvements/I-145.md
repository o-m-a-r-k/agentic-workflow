# I-145: Plans are refused for the form of their impact analysis only after the planner reports

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.11
- **Test:** `scenarios/plan-check.test.mjs`

## Failure

Audit of 51 attempts: nine plans were refused (a survey entry naming no query, a change with no suites, a file no query hit) after the planner had reported; two needed a fresh planner.

## Fix

wf plan --check runs every check wf plan runs, re-runs the plan's queries and records nothing; the planner template says to run it before reporting.
