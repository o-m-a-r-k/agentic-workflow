# I-232: The strategist picks an action but never diagnoses why the ticket loops or corrects the flow

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.34
- **Test:** `scenarios/strategy-diagnosis.test.mjs`

## Failure

At a round count or a divergence the strategist records an action, a free-text rationale and a prediction. It is told to name the cause, but nothing requires a diagnosis of what kept going wrong across the rounds, nothing requires a correction to how the work runs (not just the next fix), nothing checks at the next decision whether that correction was applied and worked, and nothing stops the same cause being met with the same course again. Field case: one attempt ran 20 review rounds, mostly re-reviewing recording evidence that the available simulator could not produce (a missing capability), plus exporter failures and a moving base; the strategist escalated once and later consolidated, and the rounds kept repeating the same unavailable-evidence cycle. The person: the strategist should look at what went wrong, step back, understand what is happening and fix the flow so the ticket does not keep looping.

## Fix

The strategist diagnoses by cause with refs that exist, accounts for every round since its previous decision, corrects how the work runs and reviews open corrections; the same lever for a cause that came back is refused.
