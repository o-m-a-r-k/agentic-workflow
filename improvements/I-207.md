# I-207: A low finding or a not-caused left item opens its own fix round

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.28
- **Test:** `scenarios/planned-pass.test.mjs`

## Failure

In a three-ticket batch, 10 of 16 review rounds of one ticket (5 of 8, 4 of 11, 7 of 10 in the others) were low-only or empty: every low finding and every implementer-reported left item (56 of 61 discovered) became an immediate implementer round, then a review round. The work skill says a low never opens a round, but nothing in the engine or the round-boundary text stops it, and the person's standing 'fix all' was used to open each round. Goal: lows and discovered items join the next pass that happens anyway, or one consolidated pass at the end; 'fix all' is recorded once as the person's decision, never re-asked and never a reason to open a round.

## Fix

What does not block joins a fix round that happens anyway, or one planned pass (one amendment grouping every outstanding item into P<n> work items, one implementer, reviewed by the evidence review before acceptance); the person's 'fix all' is recorded once from their own words and stops leave and defer questions.
