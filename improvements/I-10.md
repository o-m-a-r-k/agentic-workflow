# I-10: Lessons live in the repository they concern and ship with the ticket

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.3.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/lessons.test.mjs` (per repo: a lesson recorded in an open attempt is committed in that repo's worktree, covered (not unplanned), reviewed and delivered)

## Failure

Lessons were written into one repo's adapter folder and left uncommitted; findings about the workflow itself were stored in projects.

## Fix

Repo lessons in their repo, project lessons in the adapter repo, committed in the attempt and delivered with it; workflow findings go to the plugin improvement inbox.
