# I-141: Evidence lock replacement after a missing no-follow read is mistaken for unreadable ownership

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.12
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-0712.test.mjs` (I-141: replacement lock after ENOENT on the no-follow open is another collision, not an unreadable owner)

## Failure

The full Linux-root verifier failed the concurrent transaction probe: open returned ENOENT after release, then a separate lstat observed a replacement regular lock and caused a false unreadable-owner refusal. Deterministic host and Linux-root reproduction confirms the interleaving. Preserve the no-follow read outcome so replacement is another collision; retain all unsafe-entry, uncertain/live-owner and no-age-expiry refusals.

## Fix

one no-follow read decides: a lock missing at the read is another collision and is retried; a link, folder or hard link still refuses
