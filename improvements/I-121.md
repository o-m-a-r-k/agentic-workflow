# I-121: The owner agent deferred most findings of a delivery on its own judgement

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.7
- **Test:** `scenarios/deferral-rule.test.mjs`

## Failure

Under the severity rule a medium finding was the owner agent's call and a low one left freely, so an owner deferred 14 of 15 findings of one delivery without asking the person: a medium issue the person had explicitly asked to make work (the owner called it a product decision), and two defects rated low that touched money and customer-visible data. The person's rule is now that medium and high are always fixed in the delivery and a low one leaves only with the person's decision, all low items in one question.

## Fix

Medium and high findings and discovered issues are fixed in the delivery and refused by every placement route; a low one leaves only with the person's yes, all in one question that shows who rated each item and every re-rating; lowering into low, ejecting or abandoning holders of moved work and dropping moved criteria are the person's decisions; fixed claims on medium items wait for a reviewer.
