# I-122: The advance live-child scenario fails once under full-suite load

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.7
- **Test:** `scenarios/advance.test.mjs`

## Failure

scenarios/advance.test.mjs 'claude: a recorded closure from a live child never advances; competing runner refuses and interruption preserves its receipt' failed once in a full npm test run (line 112: expected exit 1 for a competing runner, got 0, the advance started code-review) and passed in three isolated runs and the next full run. A race between the live-child check and the competing runner under machine load; either the check or the test's timing is not deterministic.

## Fix

The scenario waits for the child's recorded review until the child exits or the test's deadline nears, and asserts the marker with the child's stderr, instead of a fixed 3 s budget.
