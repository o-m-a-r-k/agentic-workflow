# I-63: Foreground advance rejects a settled unified implementer

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/advance.test.mjs` (an implementer handed without --work while work items are unstarted covers them all)

## Failure

The frozen plan allowed one full implementer for W1 and W2. A verified native implementer handed without --work completed both repositories and the owner closed it. Manual fresh review succeeded, but wf advance stopped with work items have not started because nextDecision requires h.work per item. Either refuse an unscoped implementer before launch when work items exist, or support an explicit validated multiple-work assignment and recognize its closure; do not require fabricated bookkeeping handoffs.

## Fix

An implementer handed without --work covers every unstarted work item, so a settled unified implementer leads to review.
