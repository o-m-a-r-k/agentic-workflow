# I-101: Recognize contextual adoption authorization from question-panel replies

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.3
- **Test:** `scenarios/approve-question.test.mjs` (the harness-recorded answer is the approval)

## Failure

The human selected Adopt and finish the existing fix locally in the native asynchronous question panel, after a question explicitly asking adoption of the existing attempt. The answer arrived as a human message with structured question and answer text. Adoption rejected it as lacking an affirmative unconditional request, blocking already-authorized local work. Recognize the host-recorded human answer together with its referenced question and resolved attempt, without granting authority to agent-generated options or tool outputs.

## Fix

Adopting an attempt whose owner session is live is confirmed by the person's answer in the question panel (wf approve --from-question).
