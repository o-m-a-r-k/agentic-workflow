# I-20: A delivery whose adapter observe reports a non-integrated state still counts as delivered on retry when the commit is on the target branch

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/adapter-fault.test.mjs` (I-20: an adapter that merged and then reports ci-failed)

## Failure

If an adapter's integrate pushes straight to the target branch and observe then reports anything other than integrated (for example CI that fails after the merge, or a missing state), the next wf deliver counts the repo as delivered from the git ancestry check alone and never asks the adapter again. The engine should record what the adapter last reported, surface a non-integrated post-merge state to the owner in the delivery output, and make the owner acknowledge it (like deferrals), instead of silently treating ancestry as the whole proof.

## Fix

wf deliver records the last state the delivery adapter reported (repo.integrating) and asks the adapter again on every retry, also when the change is already on the target branch; a pending state waits and says the change is on the target; any other state after the merge (ci-failed, rejected, undocumented, none) refuses until the owner acknowledges that repo and that exact state with --acknowledge-adapter-state <repo>:<state>; the state and the acknowledgement are recorded with the delivery and printed under 'not a clean adapter delivery'.
