# I-17: Lessons are injected outside the scope of the change

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.4.3
- **Test:** `scenarios/lessons.test.mjs` (lessons are matched by their declared scope)

## Failure

UI lessons of one repo and a project lesson about another subject were injected into a quick fix that changed only scripts in another repo, because a lesson without paths matched any admitted repo; the reviewer had to give each a verdict.

## Fix

A lesson declares repo, components, paths and kinds and is injected only when a changed (or planned) file satisfies all of them or the ticket mentions one of its tags; an admitted but unchanged repo no longer counts for the reviewer, a lesson without scope matches by tags only (paths ** for every change); filtered ids are printed and ledgered, wf lesson preview says why.
