# I-196: Delivery has no deployment step: a label route can only set the final status at merge, before the deployment is confirmed and the fix tested

- **Class:** tracker
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.27
- **Test:** `scenarios/deployment-confirmation.test.mjs`

## Failure

A project's hotfix tickets end delivery with the final status (Done), but only after the deployment of the merged change is confirmed and the fix is tested in the deployed environment where possible. Merging deploys automatically, minutes later. The workflow's last tracker event is delivered, which fires when the accepted commit is on the target branch, so a statusByLabel route to Done sets it at merge, before anything is deployed or tested. There is no step to confirm deployment, record what was tested (or why it could not be), and only then set the routed status; the agent does it by hand outside the workflow or not at all.

## Fix

delivery.deployment makes a delivery wait after the merge, for every ticket or for labelled ones, until the merged change is confirmed running (the delivery adapter's read-only deployment check, or the agent's recorded observation) and the fix is tested there or why not is recorded; then the deployed tracker step posts its comment and sets its routed status, and the attempt closes. The step and its routes are read from one config version.
