# I-142: Explicit exclusion of legacy high discovered risks has no reserved disposition route

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.12
- **Test:** `scenarios/decisions-per-item.test.mjs`

## Failure

<ticket>: the user explicitly directed leaving a legacy module unchanged, and accepted amendment 2 freezes that legacy compatibility boundary. D4 and D13 honestly remain high and open. wf scope defer --finding D4,D13 refuses leave:medium-high because admission policy is high:never; scope waive only accepts open review findings, and the round deliver-core exception cannot be created for an ordinary product scope decision. Do not force changes to that module, mark these fixed, lower ratings or manufacture review rounds. Provide an auditable human-reserved disposition for existing discovered risks excluded by explicit product direction, preserving severity and reviewer visibility.

## Fix

with --person-directed, a high or medium item the person directs out of the ticket becomes their own per-item question and leaves only on their answer
