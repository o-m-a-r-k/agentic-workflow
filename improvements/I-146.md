# I-146: A strategist's escalation reaches the person as unreadable workflow prose

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.11
- **Test:** `scenarios/strategist.test.mjs`

## Failure

A strategist escalation reached the person as its own rationale with options like authorize-policy-correction, no description and no recommendation; the person could not tell what was asked.

## Fix

An escalation carries a plain question, 2-3 options each with a plain label and what follows, and a recommendation when there is one; the panel shows them, the person answers by label, the engine keeps stable choice ids; the round escalation speaks plainly too.
