# I-74: Historical runtime origin causes repeated provider approvals after adoption

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.16
- **Test:** `scenarios/runtime-preservation.test.mjs` (owning-host defaults, persistent approved switches and ownership-bound launch checks)

## Failure

An adopted attempt retained its admission provider and demanded another literal runtime receipt for every repair handoff, even when the receiving host already owned the attempt.

## Fix

Inherit the recorded current owning host automatically. Persist an explicitly approved provider selection within that ownership period, require a new receipt for another switch, and reset the selection on adoption. Bind selections to their approving owner so late handoffs cannot transfer authority. Keep historical admission and per-handoff approvals as audit records with their original scope.
