# I-226: sharedWith on admission corrections is recorded only by the later attempt

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.33
- **Test:** `scenarios/correction-reads.test.mjs`

## Failure

Fable's re-check of 0.7.32: attempt A corrects a step, then B corrects the same step: B records sharedWith [A]; A's record stays empty and A's next step never names B, and a listed attempt that closed stays mentioned. Make the note symmetric (computed from the records of open attempts' events) or document it.

## Fix

Attempts sharing an admission correction are named live through a small per-attempt correction index extended from the ledger's new bytes and checked against its hash chain.
