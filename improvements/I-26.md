# I-26: Mandatory impact analysis before planning: query-backed inventory of affected components, flows, contracts and tests; plan refused without it

- **Class:** template
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/impact.test.mjs`

## Failure

<ticket> retrospective (deeper pass): the planner ran 4.3 minutes on Sonnet, never opened the <project>, and its bundle's impact field was empty; 16 of 25 items added later and 21 of 41 review findings were visible by queries on the base commits (tables without phone cards 100/128, error branch replacing the table 41 files, empty-page early return 18, raw enum cells 28, invoice update path releasing billed work logs, public <project> tests asserting the old schema). Required: the planner output gets an impact block (changed symbols with their consumers, a per-affected-component row covering endpoint and limit, write paths, paging kind, empty/loading/error/permission/mobile/RTL/public-<project> behaviour, sorting/reorder, client-side totals, raw enums, existing tests; end-to-end flows incl. failure paths; contracts crossed; suites that must run; pattern list with queries); every list comes from a recorded query with its hit count; wf plan re-runs the queries and refuses a plan whose counts do not match; every hit maps to a work item or a stated reason; adding an endpoint, error code, DTO or repo mid-attempt requires an impact update before an implementer starts; the reviewer bundle includes the impact map and the reviewer re-runs the pattern queries on the final tree and samples at least 10 inventory entries; findings outside the map are tagged impact-gap and counted. Remove 'keep the scope to the issue; note follow-ups separately' from the planner template: discovered issues are fixed in the ticket, never deferred. Planner role defaults to the strongest model for cross-cutting work.

## Fix

The planner writes a survey before the design and an impact map after it; every list names a structured query (pattern, kind literal or regex, repo, path globs) the engine runs itself with size caps and no shell, and wf plan re-runs each, refusing wrong counts, uncovered survey entries, impact entries citing nothing in the design and must-run suites banned by doNotRun (required for full-class work, adapter impact.requiredFor). A scope-adding amendment owes an impact addendum before the next implementer handoff. The reviewer bundle carries the map, an inventory to sample and the callers the final diff reaches outside it; the closure needs impactChecked; impact-gap findings are counted in wf report. Planner role text no longer limits scope: discovered issues are fixed in the ticket. Planner and reviewer default to the review class, which pins opus.
