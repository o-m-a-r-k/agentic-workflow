# I-151: Live attempts keep the adapter they were admitted with

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.30
- **Test:** `scenarios/adapter-follow.test.mjs`

## Failure

A ticket admitted before the adapter scoped its evidence glob kept counting 4,008 other tickets' screenshots; another project's attempt kept a leaving policy its project had changed. Changes delivered on the base branch do not reach live attempts.

## Fix

A live attempt follows the adapter committed at its adapter repo's base tip (never a worktree or unpushed commit), moving forward only when a file it reads changed, recorded as adapter.followed; criteria, plan, own checks and handoffs stay pinned; a passed gate stops counting only when the move changed what it ran, and the next gate reruns just that.
