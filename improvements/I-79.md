# I-79: Replacement implementer loses stopped handoff sweeps

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.4
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-074-lifecycle.test.mjs` (a repair handoff stopped before it ran does not spend its sweeps)

## Failure

handoff implementer computes already-swept findings from every implementer handoff after the latest review, including stopped and never-launched handoffs. It rejects a replacement sweep file as no findings owed, but a replacement without --sweep does not inherit the recorded sweeps or finding details. <ticket> handoff124 stopped before launch; handoff125 omitted S44–S50. Require replacement implementers to receive unresolved sweep obligations from stopped/failed handoffs, preserving original immutable queries and recorded history, or allow supported explicit reassignment. Recovery recorded all outstanding repairs as an open discovery referencing unchanged handoff124.

## Fix

a handoff closed stopped or failed spends no sweep, so the replacement's corrected --sweep is handed, and the next reviewer's bundle leaves out a stopped handoff's unanswered sweep; the dead familiesOf(owed) is removed
