# I-66: Require owner scope decision for unrelated review findings

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.12
- **Test:** `scenarios/scope-decisions.test.mjs`

## Failure

Replace automatic fix-everything expansion with an owner scope decision. Classify findings as change-related, unrelated-existing, or uncertain using base/current evidence and causality; unchanged files and impact-map gaps are not proof of unrelatedness. Keep introduced regressions and necessary consumer repairs in scope. Report unrelated defects and impact, explicitly state repair increases scope, then ask: expand and fix, create a ticket for later, or ignore for this attempt. Wait for the direct human choice; silence does not authorize expansion. Support continue fixing all as explicit permission confined to the current attempt. Expand only after approved criteria/impact amendments. Ticket selection authorizes issue creation and requires verified tracker readback; failed or unavailable tracking remains pending. Ignore records an owner waiver and risk without claiming a fix. Ticketed and ignored unrelated findings must stop blocking repair/review loops; preserve change-related blockers, required test execution, truthful failed-gate evidence, immutable evidence, and blind review. Update planner/implementer/reviewer templates, work/quick-fix skills, lifecycle/resume/advance and acceptance/delivery consistently; supersede automatic scope-expansion rules. Scenario tests must cover a tooling-only change encountering existing portal warnings, related consumer regressions, uncertain causality, unanswered choice, all three outcomes, ticket failure, attempt-limited blanket permission, and no repeated repairs after ticket/ignore.

## Fix

Classify findings by causal scope; require direct owner choice for unrelated defects while preserving all in-scope repairs, gates and independent review.
