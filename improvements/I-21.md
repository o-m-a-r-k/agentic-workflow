# I-21: No recovery when a delivery adapter fault hits a partly delivered multi-repo attempt

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/adapter-fault.test.mjs` (several repos, one delivered, then the adapter faults)

## Failure

An attempt is pinned to the delivery adapter as committed at admission. When the adapter reports an undocumented state after another repo of the same attempt was already delivered, wf deliver refuses on every retry, wf abandon refuses because the attempt is partly delivered, and a fixed adapter on the base branch never reaches it. Needs a supported recovery (for example an owner step that re-pins the adapter base with a recorded reason, or delivering the remaining repos through a new attempt).

## Fix

wf deliver --repin-adapter [<tip>] --reason re-reads the delivery adapter of an attempt that has not delivered everything from the tip of the base branch it lives on (fetched, never the working tree), only forward along that branch and only when adapter files changed; without the tip it refuses and shows the owner both pins, the changed adapter files, what is delivered or skipped and what is left; with the tip typed back it records adapter.repinned (old and new pin, reason) and delivers the rest; gate, review and tracker stay on the admission pin; wf abandon names delivered and skipped repos and points to the re-pin.
