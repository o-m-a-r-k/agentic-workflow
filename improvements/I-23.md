# I-23: Review handoff must require a green gate on the tree and no open implementer handoff; record why a gate is stopped

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/review-order.test.mjs` (wf stop needs a reason class; the ledger records the class and the minutes of discarded gate work), `scenarios/implementer-open.test.mjs` (the reviewer handoff refuses while an implementer handoff is open)
- **Commit:** fb57a2b

## Failure

<ticket> retrospective: 16 gates were started and 15 stopped, none finished (about 175 of 271 gate minutes discarded); reviewers ran in parallel with unfinished gates so no closure ever saw a passed gate or any screenshot; two review rounds were refused because implementers were still editing; and a stale public <project> test that a finished gate would have caught reached round 15. Engine changes: wf handoff reviewer refuses (or requires a recorded reason) unless a gate has passed on the current tree or is running on it, and while any implementer handoff of the attempt is still open; wf stop requires a reason class (major finding, tree change, owner decision) and records the minutes of discarded gate work; refused review rounds leave a ledger event.

## Fix

Superseded ordering per I-27 (code review needs no gate; only the evidence review follows a passed gate). wf stop needs --class major-finding|tree-change|owner-decision and records discardedMinutes and stepsFinished; a closure refused for a tree change leaves a review.refused event; the reviewer handoff refuses while a gate runs. The reviewer handoff refuses while any implementer handoff of the attempt is open: an implementer handoff writes `implementer.opened` and stays open until `wf handoff close --agent <id>` (`implementer.closed`, outcome done, stopped or failed), so an implementer that was never closed blocks; the implementer handoff output, `wf resume` and the work and quick-fix skills say to close it; `wf handoff reviewer --reason "<why>"` hands the tree anyway and records `review.override` (reason, open implementers, reviewer) before the round. Ledgers from before 0.5.0 have no implementer events and are not blocked
