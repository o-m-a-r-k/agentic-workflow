# I-52: Quiet native role launches cause duplicate owner transcript monitoring

- **Class:** skill
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.7
- **Test:** `scenarios/role-progress.test.mjs`

## Failure

Owners repeatedly read an in-flight role transcript and restate interim findings to report liveness. Emit bounded live-process status and instruct owners to wait on the original execution handle and process the final result once.

## Fix

Emit compact live-process stderr liveness at most once per minute; guide owners to bounded waits on the original handle and one terminal import without interim transcript scans.
