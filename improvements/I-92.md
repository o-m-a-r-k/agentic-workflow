# I-92: Expose owner stop reason and do not retry unfinished-tree check after a tree-change stop

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/gate.test.mjs` (a check stopped for a tree change says why, and is not retried on the unchanged tree)

## Failure

On <ticket> native43 reported that the stopped wf check record gave no reason, then retried wf check despite related D61 and approved D60 remaining source fixes. Root supported stop was accepted at zero minutes; retry queued on live unrelated Docker lease and native eventually cancelled only its waiter. Preserve actual stop class/reason in role readback and finish admitted source before expensive final-tree checks.

## Fix

A stopped check records its class and reason, and a retry on the unchanged tree after a tree-change or major-finding stop is refused.
