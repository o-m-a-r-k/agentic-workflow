# I-92: Expose owner stop reason and do not retry unfinished-tree check after a tree-change stop

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/gate.test.mjs` (a check stopped for a tree change says why, and is not retried on the unchanged tree)

## Failure

A native implementer reported that the stopped wf check record gave no reason, then retried wf check although two source fixes it was handed were still open. The stop was accepted at zero minutes; the retry queued on an unrelated running lease, and the implementer finally cancelled only its own wait. The stop's class and reason should reach the role, and admitted source should be finished before an expensive whole-tree check.

## Fix

A stopped check records its class and reason, and a retry on the unchanged tree after a tree-change or major-finding stop is refused.
