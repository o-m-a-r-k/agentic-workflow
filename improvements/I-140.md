# I-140: Native handoff wait uses role age instead of a new wait interval

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.12
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-0712.test.mjs` (I-140: `handoff wait --minutes N` on a role already past its own estimate waits N more minutes)

## Failure

After a native implementer returned control at its own 30-minute estimate, handoff wait with --minutes 10 returned immediately because it compared ten minutes against total role age. The work skill describes N as how much longer the owner expects to wait. A fresh bounded wait should measure its own interval, or the CLI and skill must explicitly document that the option is a total elapsed deadline. Continuing with --minutes 40 supplied the intended ten additional minutes without restarting or polling the role.

## Fix

wait --minutes counts N more minutes from the call; a bare wait on a role past its own estimate follows it to its end
