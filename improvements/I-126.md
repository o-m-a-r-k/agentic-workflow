# I-126: The person could not read or partly answer the leaving question

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.8
- **Test:** `scenarios/decisions-per-item.test.mjs`

## Failure

The person was asked one yes/no question about three low findings at once, written in the reviewers' long text: they could not understand it, could not fix one and let another go, and their written answer ('fix all here, except F3 if it is in <ticket>') was not a choice, so the decision stayed open. They also found the workflow too tight: 0.7.7 asked them about ratings, ejections and dropped criteria they never wanted to decide.

## Fix

Each item that would leave is its own short question (let it leave / fix now) in a plain line; an answer in the person's own words is recorded as theirs; wf decisions withdraw retires a changed question; the adapter's leaving: and severity: let the project decide; 0.7.7's defensive questions and delivery stops are removed.
