# I-106: The person could confirm no reserved decision away from the machine

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.3
- **Test:** `scenarios/approve-question.test.mjs` (the harness-recorded answer is the approval)

## Failure

wf approve needed the person's own terminal. Working from the phone through the harness's remote control, the person could confirm nothing: an attempt whose owner session still read as alive could not be adopted by a new session, and the ticket stopped until they reached the machine.

## Fix

wf approve <id> --from-question reads the person's answer from the agent session's question panel as the harness recorded it (Claude Code AskUserQuestion answers, Codex request_user_input replies bound to their call). The question must name the attempt and decision and carry its summary, offer exactly its choices and be answered after the decision; prose, sub-agents and other sessions are never read. Refusals print the exact question to ask.
