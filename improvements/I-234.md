# I-234: Course signals fired without a strategist being handed for ten rounds

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.34
- **Test:** `scenarios/strategy-diagnosis.test.mjs`

## Failure

Between the round-10 question and the person's hold at round 20, one attempt ran eleven handed rounds with no strategist decision, while a fix chain (each low wording fix causing the next finding, four in a row) and base drift were visible. The owner agent judges the course at each round boundary and hands the strategist when it diverges; it did not. Check which course signals fired in that span and whether an unanswered signal should hand the strategist by itself, and add the signal for a round that re-raises a finding a strategist correction's refs already cover, without sameCauseAs.

## Fix

Course signals that are facts of divergence (rounds past an agent's own expectation, regression, confirmed recurrence, a re-raised diagnosed finding) hold the next implementer and code-review round until a strategist decides.
