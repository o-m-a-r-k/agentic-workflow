# I-42: Historical implementer report scanner blocks review on resolved work and coordination statements

- **Class:** guard
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.4
- **Test:** `scenarios/report-resolution.test.mjs`

## Failure

Reviewer handoff scans every historical Claude implementer final report, including completed earlier repair rounds. The UNFIXED regex flags lines such as Both follow-ups are done and committed and Final integration is done and wf check passed, and coordination notes about leaving another active implementer files alone. It requires a discovered D id in the immutable old report even when current ledger fixes and later checks prove resolution. Reconcile structured current obligations and resolution evidence, distinguish completed work and concurrency ownership from an unfixed defect, and add scenario tests preserving real unresolved-defect rejection without requiring transcript edits.

## Fix

Reconcile completed and coordination clauses and exact later ledger resolutions; preserve unresolved defect and mixed-clause refusal without transcript edits.

Historical statements superseded by a later completed implementation in the same scope are protected reviewer observations. Every one needs a current-tree verdict; fixed commits are validated, nonissues require purpose-based rationale, and open findings block the gate and delivery. Transcript edits and old verdicts supply no authority.
