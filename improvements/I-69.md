# I-69: Native reviewers terminate with unfinished whole-change review and no closure

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.4
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-074-lifecycle.test.mjs` (a closure with unreached scope is not the full audit)

## Failure

Two fresh native code-review launches completed process exit zero but stopped before whole-change review. First wrote a rejected incomplete closure; next wrote none. They consumed supplied data in bounded batches but ended while required diff and impact review remained. Launcher has no supported continuation for unfinished same-round work and forbids relaunch, so automatic fresh rounds discard all partial progress. Preserve blind provenance and validity checks while adding a supported bounded continuation path or explicit persistence instructions; never accept a partial closure or narrow scope.

## Fix

a launched reviewer that ends with no closure is told the real routes (record a complete closure file, or hand a fresh reviewer; a handoff launches once) and that the round is not an audit; a closure naming unreached scope no longer counts as the full audit (0.7.4 attempts) and notReached is shown in resume, signals, export, the delivered comment and the next reviewer's bundle; the reviewer role ends with a recorded closure
