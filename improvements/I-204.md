# I-204: Batch creation drops member supplemental proof registrations

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.29
- **Test:** `scenarios/batch-validation.test.mjs`

## Failure

Four accepted deferred-heavy members were assembled into BATCH-<ticket>. Its reviewer found supplementalValidation empty and frozen gate missing the members expense HTTP/browser and approval-browser checks. Required committed proofs must remain required on the combined tree; batch creation should carry or explicitly require registration of member supplemental validations, including collected screenshots, instead of allowing an apparently complete batch gate without them.

## Fix

Batch creation carries every check a member registered or corrected, pinned at the member's commit with its screenshot globs; the batch gate runs them on the combined tree and counts only once each passed; conflicting definitions refuse batch creation; a member's corrected admission step is never deferred.
