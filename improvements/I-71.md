# I-71: Native role commentary discarded by progress reporting

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.15
- **Test:** `scenarios/codex-run.test.mjs` (native role relays agent-reported progress without leaking reasoning or changing its terminal receipt)

## Failure

Long reviews emitted useful assistant commentary and preliminary defect reports, but the launcher showed only runtime liveness. Owners could not distinguish concrete review progress from an active process without loading transcripts.

## Fix

Relay bounded, sanitized and coalesced native assistant commentary as explicitly unverified reports on stderr; preserve quiet heartbeat cadence, JSON stdout, launch receipts and review acceptance.
