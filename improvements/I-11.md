# I-11: Screenshots must be visible on the ticket, not only attached

- **Class:** tracker
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/visible-screenshots.test.mjs`

## Failure

Uploaded attachments show in the ticket only as link rows; the owner saw zero images on the ticket.

## Fix

A delivery with screenshots always posts the delivered comment that embeds them after the uploads: from the project's template or, when it names none or its delivered events attach without a comment, from a built-in template, so a ticket with the uploads alone is refused at the readback; an agent-reported readback counts an image only by the assetUrl reported for that screenshot's upload (a placeholder left in, a link or another upload's image is refused); the handoff text says an attachment alone is only a link row.
