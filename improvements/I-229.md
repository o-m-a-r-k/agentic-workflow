# I-229: Round question asked after the strategist planned and the person gave the course

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.33
- **Test:** `scenarios/strategist.test.mjs`

## Failure

The round escalation fires on the handed-round count alone. On one attempt: the round-20 question was withdrawn when the person put a hold on the attempt ('stop and rethink ... why so many rounds'), so the firing restarted; the person lifted the hold with a direction ('release the api and portal fixes and then the mobile apps'); the strategist then decided consolidate with a bounded plan; two minutes later the engine asked the same round-20 question again, recommending exactly what the person had said. The person: 'why is the strategist not reviewing this first: I ask it to finish it and deliver and i get the following message'. Fix: at the round count the strategist decides first; the person's question is asked only when the strategist escalates or its predictions keep missing; a person's own course direction since the due count (a hold lifted in their words, an answer, a recorded decision) settles the firing and the count restarts from it.

## Fix

At the round count the strategist decides first, with a prediction judged by the next count; the person is asked on escalation, and their own direction after the count settles it.
