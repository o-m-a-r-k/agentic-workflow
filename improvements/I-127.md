# I-127: Planner handoff retains analysis-only goals after implementation intent upgrade

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.9
- **Test:** `scenarios/dead-ends.test.mjs` (I-127)

## Failure

After an analysis attempt was upgraded to implementation, fresh planners received the earlier analysis-only plan without the intent or the recorded upgrade reason. An amendment carrying only `plan.summary` was accepted with no changed criteria and did not enter the next bundle. The repeated documentation plans that followed, and a full-plan versus impact-amendment mismatch, delayed the fix. Asked for: the current intent and the owner's reason in the planner's context, a supported route for the owner's context, and refusing amendment fields that would be ignored.

## Fix

Planners see the attempt's intent and its upgrade reasons; until the first implementer, wf plan replaces an analysis plan after an upgrade; wf handoff planner --context carries the person's words; an amendment refuses fields it would drop.
