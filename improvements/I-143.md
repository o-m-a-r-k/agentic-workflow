# I-143: Gate steps rerun when nothing that can change their result changed

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.11
- **Test:** `scenarios/validation-correction.test.mjs`

## Failure

Audit of 51 attempts: a step's identity hashed its whole definition, so a correction that only scoped its evidence glob reran a 25-minute e2e step; a change in the workflow's own folder (a lesson, the adapter) made the run full and reran steps of packages with no changed file.

## Fix

A step's identity leaves out what cannot change its result (evidence globs, scheduling, planning fields); a narrowed evidence glob re-selects this ticket's files from the recorded run; the workflow's own folder counts only for steps that name it; passes recorded before 0.7.11 are matched on their old terms.
