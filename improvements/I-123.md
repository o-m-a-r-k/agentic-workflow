# I-123: The delivered comment says a finding moved to a sibling that left the delivery

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.8
- **Test:** `scenarios/decisions-per-item.test.mjs`

## Failure

When the person lets the holder of a moved finding leave the batch (eject, abandon, or deferral of the moved item), the parent's known limits still say 'Follow-up moved to <sibling> for <ref>', which reads as if the sibling ships with the batch. Deliver has the sibling's state and the decision at that point; record it on the parent so the comment says the sibling left this delivery by the person's decision.

## Fix

The delivered comment says a moved finding is still open in a sibling that is not delivered with this attempt, instead of a follow-up moved.
