# I-12: Investigate anomalies seen in evidence before calling them cosmetic

- **Class:** template
- **Fixed in:** 0.5.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/review-anomalies.test.mjs`

## Failure

A cross-locale difference in screenshot numbers was dismissed as cosmetic without checking; it came from shared test data and hid vacuous assertions.

## Fix

A reviewer handed screenshots must record anomalies in its closure: 'none seen' after comparing the captures, or per anomaly the screenshots, what differs and either a finding id from its findings, or the investigated cause with its evidence and the test assertion that pins the value (a value no test pins is a finding); wf review refuses a missing key, free text, a cause without evidence or assertions, an unknown screenshot and an unknown finding; the reviewer role and bundle say so.
