# I-132: Evidence review rejects priorFindings update after accepted blind closure

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.10
- **Test:** `scenarios/review-rounds.test.mjs` (I-132)

## Failure

<ticket> evidence reviewer successfully recorded its blind round4 closure, then verified prior findings as instructed. Re-recording the same review with priorFindings was refused as if the blind closure had not been recorded. The accepted closure and evidence remain intact; a later delta review must still verify the prior findings. Recognize the successful first receipt when accepting the instructed second-phase priorFindings update.

## Fix

Once a round's blind closure is recorded, a second record adds its priorFindings; the round's own findings and historical verdicts stay as first recorded.
