# I-85: Reject page UUID admission for a tracker with STY ticket identity

- **Class:** tracker
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/lifecycle.test.mjs` (ticket id before creating anything)

## Failure

Entry accepted a Notion page UUID as an item, but the configured tracker normalized the capture to <ticket> and refused admission. Validate tracker identity before provisioning to prevent unnecessary attempts and role runs.

## Fix

wf entry checks the item against the tracker's ticket identity (built-in patterns or tracker.itemPattern) before anything is created.
