# I-113: The reason an owner stopped a check or gate is missing from status and resume

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.4
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-074-gate.test.mjs` (wf status and wf resume show the class and reason of a stopped check)

## Failure

A stopped check records its class and reason, but wf status and wf resume print only 'last check: stopped', and a check waiting for a full lease prints no lease name or holder.

## Fix

wf status and wf resume show the class and reason of the last stopped gate or check, and a check waiting for a full lease names the lease, its holders and how capacity is raised, in its output and in wf status
