# I-50: Concurrent handoff generation exposes transient unrecorded evidence to readers

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/dead-ends.test.mjs` (a handoff is recorded together with its bundle)

## Failure

An implementer reading handoff 87 while the owner generated handoffs 88 and 89 received evidence-integrity refusals for extra handoff files. Once generation finished, wf verify passed with no extra files. Serialize evidence reads against generation or atomically publish recorded handoff files so supported reads do not treat in-progress engine writes as tampering.

## Fix

A handoff's bundle is written, recorded and sealed in one evidence transaction, so a reader never sees it before it is recorded.
