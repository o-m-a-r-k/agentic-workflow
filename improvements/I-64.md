# I-64: Project-scoped lessons are filtered out without explicit paths

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.0
- **Test:** `scenarios/lessons.test.mjs` (a project lesson that declares no narrower scope applies project-wide)

## Failure

wf lesson add --project records scope project and repo null, but wf lesson preview filters that lesson out as declaring no scope unless paths or other selectors are set. Project-wide lessons should reach applicable role bundles by their project scope. <ticket> C26 explicitly requires reading and acknowledging the recorded L-4 path as a safe workaround; no lesson enforcement was applied.

## Fix

A project lesson with no repo, components, kinds, paths or tags applies to every role project-wide.
