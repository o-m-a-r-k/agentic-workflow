# I-163: Order proof consumers after isolated dependency producers

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.30
- **Test:** `scenarios/step-order.test.mjs`

## Failure

Full gate <ticket> run 2026-10-15T17-47-19-587Z-20395 started browser-only proof steps while Docker admission producers waited for shared capacity. <name>-browser failed its dependency-key precondition in 0.2 seconds just after symfony-unit started; symfony-unit later passed and prepared the expected tree. Model prerequisite readiness or sequence producer preparation before frozen proof consumers so lease availability cannot reorder consumers ahead of their dependencies. Preserve all checks and input pins.

## Fix

A step declares needs: [ids]; the scheduler never starts a consumer before its producers finish, and records not-started with the reason when a producer failed.
