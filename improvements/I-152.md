# I-152: A long reviewer's findings are lost when it is stopped

- **Class:** engine
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.12
- **Test:** `scenarios/fix-074-lifecycle.test.mjs` (I-152: a launched reviewer that dies after writing findings has them kept as a partial round)

## Failure

Audit of role launches: a reviewer that ran 12 hours on a 1,303-file change and one stopped after 94 minutes kept their findings only in their transcripts; nothing was saved as partial work.

## Fix

a launched reviewer that fails or is stopped has the findings it wrote kept as a partial round, never an audit or an acceptance; reviewers write findings as they find them
