# I-217: A fix was recommended and built without tracing what the data feeds; it was reverted after the person approved it

- **Class:** template
- **Fixed in:** 0.7.31
- **Test:** `scenarios/premise.test.mjs`

## Failure

In a batch delivery the owner recommended to the person, and the person approved, a change to an invoice due date (a calendar day in the seller's timezone, a new failure for an unprojected due day, a DTO reshape) based on a standards-and-display report alone. Nobody traced the field: nothing in production writes billing_invoice.due_at (only tests set it), card renewals are charged off the subscription period end with no due date, and dunning keys off the first failed charge. The change was implemented, then a second question asked the person to undo it, costing a round and a revert. Nothing in the workflow requires a recommendation or a fix design to name what was traced (producers and consumers of the data in production paths, the business process, external systems) and what was not.

## Fix

Every role establishes a finding's premise in production paths before recommending or building (writers, readers, business process, external systems, behaviour when absent) and says what it did not trace; a contradicting fact is a stop. Implementers report premises and premiseFailed (built nothing); reviewers have a verified-nonissue for a false premise; a contract change or new failure path from a non-blocking item is a re-plan, and the reach course signal hands it to the strategist; product recommendations are recorded with their basis (wf decide); owner context goes through the ledger.
