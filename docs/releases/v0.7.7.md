## 0.7.7 - Medium and high findings are fixed in the delivery; a low one leaves only with the person's yes

- I-121 (D17, supersedes D10's medium and low clauses): an owner agent deferred 14 of 15 findings of one delivery on its
  own judgement, among them a behaviour the person had asked for and two money and customer-data defects rated low.
  - A review finding or discovered issue that is high or medium (unrated, citing a frozen criterion or breaking a
    critical invariant counts as high) is fixed in this delivery, here or in a sibling attempt of the batch. `wf scope
    defer`, `wf scope ticket`, `wf discovered close --deferred` and the strategist's `ticket` refuse it, with a reason or
    without.
  - A low item leaves only with the person's yes. `wf scope defer` and `wf scope ticket` take review findings and
    discovered issues together (`--finding r1:F2,D3`), one question for the items named together. The question shows
    each item's severity, who rated it, every re-rating with its reason and where it is, and names every other open low
    item, so the owner puts them all in one question. `wf scope ticket` asks before the follow-up ticket exists (a `no`
    leaves no orphaned tracker issue); with the yes, the owner creates it and records its readback with `--capture`.
  - The routes around it are closed too (independent review of the draft): lowering a rating into low is the person's
    decision; ejecting or abandoning an attempt that holds work moved to it from another (`wf scope move`, a split) is
    the person's decision; dropping or rewording a criterion a split moved is the person's decision; a medium (as a high)
    observation claimed fixed with `wf scope fixed` waits for a reviewer's `fixed`; a discovered issue claimed fixed needs
    a commit made after it was recorded, and the next reviewer is handed it (`fixedSinceReview`) to verify; a medium or
    high one closed fixed after the last review is not delivered until a round sees the fix, and a low one is listed in
    the delivered comment's known limits as not verified by a reviewer. A moved item counts as held by its sibling
    whatever its status (a fix in a branch that leaves the batch is not delivered), and delivery refuses a `moved`
    disposition whose sibling is not in it.
- I-122: a scenario that waited a fixed 3 s for a live child under a full parallel run now waits until the child exits
  or the test's deadline.
  - A finding that blocks never leaves through a deferral or a ticket (the person may still waive it). The person's
    `deliver-core` answer to the round escalation stays the one exception.
  - The role and skill texts say so: the default is to fix; a fix that needs a product decision is a question for the
    person, not a deferral; a defect touching money, authorization, data shown to another party or legal output is never
    rated low.
- Align the CLI package and both host plugin manifests at 0.7.7.

Regression scenarios: `scenarios/deferral-rule.test.mjs` (medium and high refused by every route; low items of both kinds
in one question with who rated each, a `no` lets nothing leave, a `yes` closes them; lowering into low asks; the other open
low items are named; a fix commit older than the issue is refused; the strategist cannot queue a medium item; move, eject
and abandon ask), and `scenarios/split.test.mjs` (dropping a moved criterion asks); each fails on 0.7.6. The scenarios that
encoded D10 now check D17.
