## 0.7.7 - Medium and high findings are fixed in the delivery; a low one leaves only with the person's yes

- I-121 (D17, supersedes D10's medium and low clauses): an owner agent deferred 14 of 15 findings of one delivery on its
  own judgement, among them a behaviour the person had asked for and two money and customer-data defects rated low.
  - A review finding or discovered issue that is high or medium (unrated, citing a frozen criterion or breaking a
    critical invariant counts as high) is fixed in this delivery, here or in a sibling attempt of the batch. `wf scope
    defer`, `wf scope ticket`, `wf discovered close --deferred` and the strategist's `ticket` refuse it, with a reason or
    without.
  - A low item leaves only with the person's yes. `wf scope defer` and `wf scope ticket` take review findings and
    discovered issues together (`--finding r1:F2,D3`), so every low item is put to the person in one question; the
    question shows each item's severity and any down-rating.
  - A finding that blocks never leaves through a deferral or a ticket (the person may still waive it). The person's
    `deliver-core` answer to the round escalation stays the one exception.
  - The role and skill texts say so: the default is to fix; a fix that needs a product decision is a question for the
    person, not a deferral; a defect touching money, authorization, data shown to another party or legal output is never
    rated low.

in one question, a `no` lets nothing leave, a `yes` closes them under that decision); each fails on 0.7.6. The scenarios
that encoded D10 now check D17.
