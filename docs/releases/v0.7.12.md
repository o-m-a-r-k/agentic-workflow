## 0.7.12 - Kept findings, reused passes, and roles read as they are

The rest of the issues raised by the audit of role launches and of the refusal log in 0.7.11 (D22).

- **I-152: a reviewer that does not finish keeps what it found.**
  - Reviewers write each finding into their closure file as soon as they have it.
  - When a launched reviewer fails or is stopped, the engine records the findings it wrote so far as a partial round. Its
    unfinished scope is named in `notReached`.
  - A partial round owes nothing it did not get to (verdicts on earlier findings, screenshot anomalies). What it missed
    joins its `notReached`.
  - A partial round is never an audit and never an acceptance. The next round covers everything it did not reach: a full
    round, or a delta from the last round that finished. `wf accept` names the route to a fresh reviewer.
  - One reviewer had run 12 hours, and another was stopped after 94 minutes; both kept their findings only in their
    transcripts.
- **I-150: a pass from another attempt is reused.** A step that keeps no evidence files reuses a pass that another
  attempt of the project recorded under the same key (inputs, runner and the trees of the other repos it reads). The tree
  that run saw must also differ from this one in no package file that the step's inputs leave out. The reused record
  names the attempt it came from, and its log is copied into this attempt's evidence, verified by hash, so releasing the
  other attempt loses no proof. 163 reruns in the audit had a key another attempt had already passed. On the open
  attempts of two projects, 10 more steps are reused and none that 0.7.11 reused now runs.
- **I-142: a high or medium item leaves only on the person's own answer.** When the person directs that an item stays
  out of the ticket, the owner asks with `--person-directed "<their words>"`. The item leaves on that one answer, listed
  as a known issue they chose, with their words in the delivered list. An agent can never let it go.
- **I-137: a finished implementer is not "working".**
  - An implementer whose launch ended, but which the owner has not closed, now shows two numbers: how long it ran, and
    how long it has waited for the owner since.
  - Owner idle time no longer counts as an implementer passing its estimate.
  - `wf resume` leads with closing it.
- **I-139: a repair made before its record closes.** An implementer that repaired a bookkeeping item and then recorded
  it can close it with the commit that repaired it. This works when the ledger and git show all of these:
  - the entry is the implementer's own low bookkeeping item;
  - it was recorded while that implementer's handoff was open;
  - the commit is in the handoff's own range and touches the file the entry names.

  Defects, invariants and anything else keep the old refusal, which now says why the repair rule did not apply.
- **I-140: `wf handoff wait`.**
  - `--minutes N` counts N more minutes from the call. It used to count from the launch, so on an older role it returned
    at once.
  - A bare `wait` on a role already past its own estimate follows it to its end.
- **I-141: evidence lock race.** A lock that was released and taken again between the collision and the read is another
  collision, not an unreadable lock. Links, folders and hard links at the lock path are still refused.
