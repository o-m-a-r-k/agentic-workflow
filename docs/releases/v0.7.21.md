## 0.7.21 - A quick fix with a ticket moves the ticket

- **The tracker steps follow the ticket, not the lane.** A project worked its tracker's tickets through the quick lane.
  The engine read the quick lane as "no ticket", so those tickets never moved to In progress or to the delivered status,
  and never got the tester's comment. Now a quick attempt admitted with `--item` gets the same tracker steps as the
  standard lane, as the project's adapter defines them:
  - the item is checked against the tracker's id pattern at entry;
  - admission reads the ticket and moves it to its started status;
  - the first implementer marks it implementing;
  - delivery needs the tester's summary and hands off with the delivered status, the comment, its screenshots and the
    readback, instead of closing at once;
  - a reopened attempt reports it.

  A quick fix with no ticket, which `wf` numbers itself (`QF-<n>`), still has no tracker steps and closes on delivery.
  Whether an item is `wf`'s own number is recorded at admission and kept through reopen and split, so a tracker ticket
  that happens to be named `QF-7` is still a ticket. The quick-fix skill and the lifecycle docs say how to admit a quick
  fix on a ticket (`wf entry --item <ticket> --lane quick`).
- **Live attempts.** A quick attempt already open with a ticket follows this at its next step. Its delivery now asks for
  the summary (`wf deliver --summary-file <summary.md>`) and ends in the tracker handoff, so its ticket moves to the
  delivered status with the comment even though it never showed In progress.
