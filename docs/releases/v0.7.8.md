## 0.7.8 - One short question per item, answered your way; less in the person's way

- I-126 (D18): the person was asked one yes/no question about three items at once, in the reviewers' long text, and could
  neither understand it nor fix one and let another go; their written answer left the question open. They also found 0.7.7
  too tight.
  - Each item that would leave a delivery is its own question with two choices, `let it leave` and `fix now`, in one plain
    line (`--explain <file.json>` gives the owner's wording and what fixing it takes; else the first sentence of the
    finding) with its severity, who rated it and where. The person answers each, together in one panel.
  - An answer in the person's own words is recorded as theirs (choice `other`, with the words) and the owner follows it;
    `wf decisions withdraw <id> --reason` takes back a question whose subject changed.
  - The adapter may change who decides each level: `leaving: { high, medium, low }` (never, person or owner; by default
    high and medium never leave, a low one leaves with the person's answer), and `severity:` adds the project's own
    examples to the scale every role reads.
  - Removed: asking the person to lower a rating into low, to eject or abandon an attempt that holds moved work, or to drop
    a criterion a split moved; delivery no longer stops on moved work or on fixes closed after the last review, and a
    medium observation claimed fixed settles again. The delivered comment's known limits say so instead.
- I-123: a finding moved to a sibling that is not delivered with this attempt is listed as still open there, not as a
  follow-up moved.
- I-119: `wf resume` names the commits the base gained that no workflow delivery covered (merged by hand, so no gate
  passed them): a gate failure may come from the base.
- I-124: `wf handoff close` of an implementer checks for uncommitted work only in its own work item's repos; another
  implementer's work in progress elsewhere no longer holds it up (the review handoff still needs the whole tree committed).
- I-125: a check frozen at admission that is itself wrong can be corrected with `wf validation correct --steps <id>`, as
  the person's decision (kind `override-gate`); the original definition stays in the record, and a fresh review and the
  full gate follow, as for a supplemental correction.
- Align the CLI package and both host plugin manifests at 0.7.8.

Regression scenarios: `scenarios/decisions-per-item.test.mjs` (per-item questions and mixed answers; `--explain`; own words
and withdrawal; the adapter's `leaving:` and `severity:`; a rating into low is a reason; known limits for unverified fixes
and a departed sibling; the base warning); each fails on 0.7.7. The scenarios that encoded 0.7.7's gates check 0.7.8.
