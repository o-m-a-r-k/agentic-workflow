## 0.7.17 - A delivered comment the tester can read

- **The delivered comment's known limits are written for the tester.** One delivered comment carried 53 lines of known
  limits. 25 of them were per-round reviewer notes with file paths and test counts; the rest were deferrals with their ids,
  ratings, reasons and who decided. The section now says, in a few lines:
  - how many items the final review left unchecked;
  - each known issue that is high, medium or left out at the person's direction, by its first sentence;
  - how many minor known issues were left for later;
  - fixes no reviewer saw, follow-up tickets and moves, each by what the item is rather than its id.

  The full wording, ids, ratings and who decided stay in the delivery output and `wf export`. On that ticket it is 7 lines.
- **A known issue reads in the owner's plain words when they gave them.** The plain line from `--explain` (the one the
  person was asked about) is kept on the decision, and the delivered comment names the item by it. Reviewers' finding
  summaries and implementers' `left` items start with a plain sentence a tester would understand, with file and test
  detail after it.
- **A comment worded differently is changed through the summary, and edited in place.** When the person wants the comment
  worded differently, the owner changes the summary (`wf summary --file`) and posts the new rendering: a hand-edited comment
  fails the readback. When a comment is already posted, the readback refusal says to edit that comment to the rendering in
  place, which it accepts, so the ticket keeps one comment.
