## 0.5.16 - Inherit the owning runtime

- Fresh handoffs and recovery inherit the current recorded owning host. Adoption into Codex or Claude uses that host automatically; admission runtime remains audit history.
- An explicit approved provider switch is remembered for later handoffs and recovery under the same owner. Another switch requires a new approval; adoption resets the choice to the new owning host.
- Selections are bound to the approving owner, including handoffs recorded after an ownership transfer. Historical per-handoff approvals retain their original scope, and native launch continues to reject unauthorized provider changes.

Regression scenarios cover both hosts, repeated implicit and explicit handoffs after adoption, recovery with an unrelated caller runtime, persistent approved switches, switch-back approval, invalid receipts, legacy grants and late handoffs after adoption.
