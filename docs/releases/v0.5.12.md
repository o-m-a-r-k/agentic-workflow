## 0.5.12 - Owner decisions for unrelated findings

- I-66: review and implementation findings distinguish requested work and regressions from independent pre-existing defects. Missing or uncertain scope, changed contracts, required consumers and required validation remain blocking and continue through repair rounds.
- Proven unrelated findings pause for a direct human choice: expand scope and fix, create follow-up tickets, or ignore for this attempt. Expansion requires updated criteria and impact before implementation. Ticket creation stays pending until a real raw tracker readback is recorded; the readback is labelled agent-reported rather than host-verified.
- Decisions follow the defect across review rounds without waiving a later in-scope classification. Blanket expansion applies only to extra findings in the current attempt. Ignoring or ticketing cannot waive a failed gate or replace independent evidence review.
- Both runtimes receive the scope checkpoint in skills and generated role instructions. Existing attempts without scope metadata remain conservative.

The scenario covers mixed scope, recurring findings, direct human authority, rejected conditional or replayed replies, ticket failures, criteria amendments and unchanged delivery guards.
